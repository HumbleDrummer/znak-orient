# Email sequence — opt-in only

These four templates assume the recipient **requested the audit themselves** (e.g. submitted their domain and email into a "get your free audit" form on a landing page) — not that their domain was scraped from a target list and emailed cold. That distinction is what keeps this sequence legal and low-risk to send:

- Under Polish law (ustawa o świadczeniu usług drogą elektroniczną) and GDPR/ePrivacy generally, sending unsolicited commercial email — even B2B — without a prior relationship or consent is legally risky in a way that "add an unsubscribe link" does not fix. A self-requested audit is the recipient's own action, which is a clean basis to email them back.
- It also converts better: someone who asked for their own audit is already interested, unlike a stranger picked off a scraped list.

If you still want to reach people who haven't opted in, that requires an actual legal review for each target market (PL/UK/DE/US all differ) before sending anything — not a template swap. That review is outside what I can do here.

Every template below is a draft. Review and personalize before sending; none of it sends itself.
