# SiteDoctor white-label partner agreement — DRAFT TEMPLATE

**This is a starting point for a negotiation, not a signable legal contract.** It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, it does not account for jurisdiction-specific requirements (this template mentions PL/UK/DE/US as example markets — each has different commercial-agency, tax, and consumer-protection rules), and neither of the parties described below is a real signatory. Have an actual lawyer in your jurisdiction review and adapt this before either party signs anything.

---

## Parties

**Provider:** [Your name/company] — provides the SiteDoctor audit technology, reports, and monitoring dashboard.

**Partner:** [Partner agency/company name] — sells SiteDoctor-powered services to its own clients under its own brand, and owns the client relationship, invoicing, payment collection, and tax compliance for those sales.

## What the Provider delivers

- API/dashboard access to run domain audits and generate reports for Partner's clients.
- White-label reports (no Provider branding visible to Partner's clients, unless Partner opts to co-brand).
- Reasonable technical support for Partner's use of the platform.

## What the Partner is responsible for

- All sales, client relationships, contracts, invoicing, VAT/sales tax, and payment collection from end clients.
- Complying with applicable email-marketing and data-protection law (e.g. GDPR, CAN-SPAM, or local equivalents) for any outreach the Partner sends using SiteDoctor-generated reports.
- Setting end-client pricing (Provider may suggest reference pricing but does not set it).

## Revenue split

- Provider receives **[40]%** of each client's recurring subscription revenue, paid [monthly/net-30], for as long as that client remains active.
- Provider receives a one-time **$[200]** setup fee per newly onboarded client, invoiced to Partner on activation.
- [ ] Optional: sub-affiliate program — a third party who refers a client to Partner receives **[20]%** of that client's recurring revenue, funded from Partner's share, Provider's share, or split — specify which.

## Term and termination

- Either party may terminate with [30/60] days' written notice.
- On termination, already-active client subscriptions [continue under revised terms / wind down over X days — specify].
- Partner may not represent itself as the Provider or claim ownership of the underlying audit technology.

- Reports are automated technical audits, not a guarantee of results, security, or legal compliance for the end client's site.
